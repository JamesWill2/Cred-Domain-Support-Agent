# Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) Calculation Rules

Equated Monthly Installments (EMI) are calculated using the reducing balance method based on the principal amount, tenure in months, and annual interest rate. The interest component remains highest during the initial months and gradually decreases as principal amortization progresses. Borrowers can restructure or modify their EMI cycle date once per financial year by submitting a written mandate. Any EMI bounce due to insufficient funds incurs an automated standard dishonor charge of INR 500 plus applicable taxes.
