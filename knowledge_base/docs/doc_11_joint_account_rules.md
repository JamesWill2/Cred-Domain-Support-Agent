# Joint Bank Account Operational Rules and Mandates

Joint accounts may be opened with operational mandates specified as Either or Survivor, Former or Survivor, or Jointly Operated by all holders. Under an Either or Survivor mandate, any account co-holder holds full authority to issue transaction withdrawals, request cards, and sign fund transfers. In the event of the demise of one holder, surviving account holders automatically assume total balance control upon presenting a certified death certificate. Any modification to operational mandate instructions or nomination registration requires unanimous physical or digital consent from all holders.
