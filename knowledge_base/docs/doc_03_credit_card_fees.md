# Credit Card Fee Structure and Billing Policy

The primary card membership carries an annual renewal fee of INR 1,500 which is waived if annual retail card spend exceeds INR 150,000. Cash advance withdrawals at domestic ATMs attract a transaction fee of 2.5 percent of the withdrawn amount subject to a minimum of INR 500. Late payment penalties range from INR 400 to INR 1,200 depending on the outstanding total billing slab. Foreign currency cross-border transactions are levied a foreign exchange markup of 3.5 percent on the gross transaction value.
