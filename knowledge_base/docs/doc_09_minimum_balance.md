# Minimum Average Balance Requirements and Penalties

Regular savings accounts maintained in metropolitan and urban branches require an average monthly balance of INR 10,000. Semi-urban and rural retail branch savings accounts stipulate a lower minimum balance threshold of INR 5,000 and INR 2,500 respectively. Failure to maintain the stipulated average balance triggers a monthly non-maintenance penalty of up to INR 350 plus statutory tax deductions. Salary accounts linked to active institutional corporate payrolls are exempt from all minimum balance stipulations.
