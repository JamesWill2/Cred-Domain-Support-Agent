# Non-Resident Indian (NRI) Account Eligibility and Rules

Non-Resident Indians and Persons of Indian Origin are legally entitled to open Non-Resident External (NRE) and Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) accounts. NRE accounts maintain foreign currency converted to rupees with complete principal and interest tax exemption and unconditional overseas repatriation rights. NRO accounts are designed to manage domestic earnings within India such as rental income, dividends, and pensions, subject to Indian tax deduction at source. Required onboarding documentation to open an NRI account includes a valid Indian passport, foreign residence visa or permit, overseas utility address proof, and passport-size photographs.
