# Prepayment Penalty and Foreclosure Rules

Floating-rate retail loans granted to individual individual borrowers carry zero prepayment penalty or foreclosure fees as per regulatory guidelines. Fixed-rate business loans subjected to early full foreclosure within eighteen months of sanction attract a charge of 2.0 percent on the outstanding principal balance. Partial prepayments are permitted up to twice each financial year provided each prepayment equals at least two standard monthly EMI amounts. Prepayment charges are waived entirely for education loan accounts regardless of whether they are floating or fixed.
