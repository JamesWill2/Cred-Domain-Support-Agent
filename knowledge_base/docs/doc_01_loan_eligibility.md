# Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type

Personal loans require a minimum monthly net income of INR 25,000 and a credit score of 700 or above for salaried professionals. Home loans are eligible for applicants aged 21 to 65 years with verifiable employment stability and a minimum property valuation appraisal. Auto loans require a valid driving license, proof of income, and a maximum debt-to-income ratio of 45 percent. Education and business loans require collateral security or an accredited co-borrower guarantee along with audited operational financial statements.
