# Bank Account Closure Guidelines and Process

To initiate an account closure, the primary account holder must submit a signed closure request form and surrender all unused check leaves and active debit cards. All outstanding dues, negative balances, and linked active automatic debit mandates must be completely settled prior to deactivation. Remaining account balances are repatriated via instant electronic fund transfer to a verified external beneficiary bank account. Account termination is confirmed via registered SMS and email notifications within three working days.
