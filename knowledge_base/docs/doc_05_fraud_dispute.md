# Fraud Dispute Resolution Process and Reporting

Cardholders and borrowers must notify the bank immediately within 48 hours of detecting any unauthorized or suspicious digital transaction. An incident ticket is registered and a temporary chargeback credit is provisioned to the affected account within seven business days pending forensic inquiry. The digital fraud monitoring team conducts transaction tracing and merchant verification to establish genuine liability. Customers will receive formal dispute findings and final settlement resolution within twenty-one calendar days of filing.
