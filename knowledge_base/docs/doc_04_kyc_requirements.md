# Know Your Customer (KYC) Document Requirements

Know Your Customer (KYC) document requirements mandate that all account applicants provide valid Proof of Identity including a Permanent Account Number (PAN) card and Aadhaar biometric authorization. Proof of Address verification accepts recent utility bills not older than three months, passport copies, or voter identification cards. High-value retail lending applications additionally mandate submission of bank statements for the past six months and latest income tax returns. Video-based customer identification process (V-CIP) can be scheduled remotely for digital onboarding verification.
