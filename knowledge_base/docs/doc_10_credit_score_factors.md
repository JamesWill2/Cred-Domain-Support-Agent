# Credit Score Impact Factors and Bureau Reporting

Repayment history constitutes thirty-five percent of an individual credit score and any late payment over thirty days negatively impacts the rating. Credit utilization ratio should ideally remain under thirty percent of the sanction limit across all open credit card facilities. Multiple hard credit inquiries initiated within a brief window for unsecured loans lower the composite bureau score. Maintaining a balanced credit mix of secured asset-backed loans and unsecured lines alongside an established credit vintage positively improves score velocity.
