# Retail Lending and Deposit Interest Rate Slabs

Floating interest rates for prime residential home loans range from 8.25 percent to 9.15 percent linked directly to the external repo benchmark rate. Fixed-rate personal unsecured loans carry annual percentage interest rates between 10.50 percent and 16.00 percent based on credit risk tiering. Term deposit slabs for fixed deposits (FD) offer 6.50 percent for twelve-month tenures and up to 7.25 percent for tenures extending beyond thirty-six months. Senior citizens qualify for an additional interest rate markup of 50 basis points across all standard fixed deposit tenures.
